How it works

Three steps, zero setup

  1. 01

    Probe

    On launch Nebula pings the media servers it knows. A daily background sweep checks all 1,494 bundled hosts and detects which ones serve video.

  2. 02

    Merge

    Shelves and search results from every online server are parsed, cleaned up and merged by title and year into one library.

  3. 03

    Play

    Hit play and the file streams directly from the fastest server holding it. Nothing passes through us.

FAQ

Questions

What is BDIX?

The Bangladesh Internet Exchange links local ISPs so traffic between them stays inside the country. Many ISPs host FTP and media servers on it, which their customers can reach at full line speed, often without using their data cap.

Does Nebula host any movies?

No. Nebula is a client, like a browser. It lists what public BDIX servers already serve and plays those files directly from them. It hosts no content and has no servers of its own.

Why can't I see anything?

BDIX servers are only reachable from Bangladeshi ISPs that peer on BDIX. On mobile data or a non-BDIX connection most servers will show as offline.

Do I need an account?

No. My List, watch history and downloads are stored on your phone only.

Which phones are supported?

Any Android phone running Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer.
